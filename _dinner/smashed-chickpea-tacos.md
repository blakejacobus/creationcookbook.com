# Smashed Chickpea Tacos

[Delish Knowledge](https://www.delishknowledge.com/smashed-chickpea-tacos/#recipe)

## Ingredients

| Serving | Ingredient | Notes |
|-|-|-|
| 1 can | Chickpeas | Drained and rinsed |
| 1 tbsp | Soy sauce |  |
| 1 tbsp | Oil |  |
| 1 tsp | Garlic powder |  |
| 1 tsp | Onion powder |  |
| 1 tsp | Chili powder |  |
| 1 tbsp | Sour cream |  |
| 2 tsp | Cornstarch |  |
| 1/8 cup | Frozen onion | Chopped |
| 1/3 cup | Cheddar | Shredded |
| 8 | Tortillas | Corn or corn/flour mix |

## Toppings

- Avocado
- Cherry tomatoes
- Salsa
- Shredded cheese
- Sour cream

## Instructions

1. Place the drained and rinsed chickpeas, soy sauce, oil, garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, scallions, and cheddar (if using) in a food processor. Pulse until combined. Alternatively, use a fork or potato masher to combine.
1. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed, then roll into about 8 balls (roughly 2 ounces each) and place in the fridge while you prepare the toppings.
1. Prepare toppings for the tacos.
1. Heat a large griddle or skillet over medium-high heat. Lightly grease the skillet with a little oil and add a chickpea ball.
1. Top with a tortilla and press down until flattened. A burger press, wide spatula, or baking sheet will also work.
1. Cook for 2-3 minutes, then flip and cook another 1-2 minutes.
1. Add toppings and serve.
