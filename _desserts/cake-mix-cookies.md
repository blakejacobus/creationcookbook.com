# Cake Mix Cookies

## Source

[Watering Can Blog](https://wateringcanblog.com/2013/08/06/quick-and-easy-cookies/)

## Ingredients

| Serving | Ingredient | Notes |
|-|-|-|
| 1 box | Cake mix | Of choice (yellow cake mix recommended) |
| 2 whole | Eggs |  |
| 1/2 cup | Baking oil |  |
| Various | Chocolate chips | Can substitute other mix-ins |

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350F.
1. Mix ingredients together and roll into 1-2 inch balls.
1. Cake for 9 minutes.
